\Cref{tab:toy} and \cref{fig:toy} summarise the results. After training, the world model is action-sensitive in 75.8\% of held-out cases, and selecting among random chunks with the evaluator's score raises the fraction of chunks with the instructed effect from 47.7\% at $N{=}1$ (chance) to 99.7\% at $N{=}32$. The untrained System~2 stays near chance, as expected from its identity dynamics. The trained proposer alone also learns to produce the instructed effect, so in this toy task search is not \emph{needed}; the experiment is designed only to show that the world model and evaluator implement a working selection signal when the dynamics are learnable from the latent state.
